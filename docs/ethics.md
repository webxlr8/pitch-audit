# Responsible use

This tool writes unsolicited sales material about real businesses, so a few rules matter.

Only use public data. Don't log in, get past paywalls or forms, or bypass bot protection such as CAPTCHAs and rate limits.

Be light on their servers. An audit loads a handful of pages once. Don't run it in a loop against one site, and respect `robots.txt` if you add any extra crawling.

Stay accurate and kind. Every number on a slide must trace to a source, and `lint_deck.py` enforces this. Criticise the website, not the people who built or run it.

Don't impersonate anyone. A deck must come from you, and must not use a prospect's branding in a way that suggests they endorse it.

Label mockups. Product screens filled with the prospect's data must say "Illustrative mockup".

Use photos you're allowed to use: the prospect's own (for a deck sent only to them), or free-licence Unsplash and Pexels photos credited in `images/CREDITS.md`. Avoid recognisable people and other companies' logos. Never generate fake photos of their business.

Keep personal data out. Beyond the business contact details a prospect publishes, don't collect or store information about individuals.

If a prospect asks you to stop contacting them, stop.
